# SPX AI

**Version 1.0.1** · Windows 64-bit

Everything [SPX](../spx/index.md) offers, with artificial intelligence
integration that automates the process, reads PDFs intelligently and runs
commands to generate pile caps and piles.

## The AI does not take part in the calculation { #a-ia-nao-entra-no-calculo }

!!! warning "Where artificial intelligence acts"

    It is worth being explicit, because it is the first question of anyone
    evaluating the product: the artificial intelligence in SPX AI acts on the
    **data input** and the **model setup** — reading a report, recognising
    piles in a plan, generating pile caps by command.

    The calculation remains the same as in SPX: Aoki-Velloso,
    Décourt-Quaresma, Teixeira, Cintra & Aoki, finite elements, NBR 6118. No
    result comes from a statistical model.

    What changes is **how long it takes to get there** — and, as a
    consequence, how much of your time is left for checking.

## When it pays for itself { #quando-ele-se-paga }

SPX AI is justified by **volume**. On a site with fifteen boreholes and eighty
pile caps, typing in the input is most of the work, and that is what the
automation eliminates.

On a small project — two boreholes, half a dozen pile caps — SPX does the job
with the same quality.
